## 📚 To Inspiring Authors

There are books that change how you think. These are the ones that have most shaped how I teach and write:

| Author | Book | Why It Stuck With Me |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jon Duckett** | *HTML and CSS: Design and Build Websites* | The most visual and approachable book to get started. I lent it out so many times I had to buy another copy. |
| **Marijn Haverbeke** | *Eloquent JavaScript* | Taught me that JavaScript can be elegant, not just functional. |
| **Kyle Simpson** | *You Don't Know JS* (series) | Forced me to look under the hood. Changed how I understand the language. |
| **Eric A. Meyer** | *CSS: The Definitive Guide* | The CSS bible. Still a reference 20 years later. |
| **Martin Fowler** | *Refactoring* | Taught me that clean code isn't a luxury, it's a necessity. |
| **Robert C. Martin** | *Clean Code* | Controversial, yet essential for understanding good practices. |
| **Dan Abramov** | *Just JavaScript* (course) | Helped me grasp the mental model of modern JavaScript. |

!!! quote "Reading other people's code"
    Learning to program is also learning to read code written by others. These authors don't just teach concepts: they teach how to think.
