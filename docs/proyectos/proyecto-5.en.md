# 📅 Pardo Agenda

!!! abstract "🐕 Your gamified personal planner"
    **Level:** 🟠 Advanced · **Author:** @urukaisk

    Personal planner with **habits, journal, XP, and streaks**. Installable as a PWA. Everything is stored locally in your browser, with no accounts or servers.

## 🎯 What you will find

- 📊 **Daily activity** with a streak chart.
- ✅ **Habit** management with day-by-day tracking.
- 📓 Personal **journal** with entries.
- ⭐ **XP system** and levels.
- 🔥 Consecutive-day **streak**.
- 📱 **Installable PWA** (works offline).
- 👤 User profile.

## 🛠 Technologies

`HTML5` · `CSS3` · `JavaScript` · `PWA` · `localStorage` · `Netlify`
