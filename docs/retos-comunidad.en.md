# 🤝 Community Challenges

!!! quote "Challenges proposed by the community"
    Alongside the official weekly challenges, the community proposes its own coding challenges. Some are easier, others harder, but they all share something in common: they were proposed by someone learning just like you.

---

## 🎯 What are community challenges?

They are challenges **proposed by readers** of the book rather than the author. They can be:

- Variations of the official challenges.
- Specific problems someone ran into at work.
- Wild ideas that popped into your head on a Sunday afternoon.

Each challenge includes the author's name (or handle) and a description. If you want to propose one, [open an issue](#proponer-reto).

## 🏆 Community solutions

If you solved a challenge, share your solution:

- **GitHub Discussions**: [Go to Discussions](https://github.com/urukaisk-maker/Libro-desarrollador-Web-Urukais-/discussions)
- **Twitter/X**: use the hashtag `#LibroDelDesarrolladorWeb`
- **CodePen/StackBlitz**: paste the link in the challenge thread

The most creative solutions will be featured on this page.

---

## 🤔 Why join in?

- **Practice what you learn**: challenges are the best way to cement concepts.
- **Get feedback**: others comment on your solution.
- **Learn from other approaches**: there is always someone who solves it more cleanly.
- **Get featured on the page**: if your challenge is accepted, your name appears here.
- **Earn XP**: counts toward your level in the [achievements system](logros.md).
