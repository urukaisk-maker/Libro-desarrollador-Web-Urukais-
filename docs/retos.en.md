# 🎯 Challenges

!!! quote "Learn by solving real problems"
    Every week a short coding challenge is published to put what you have learned into practice. Solve it at your own pace, share your solution, and compare approaches with others in the community.

---

## 🎯 What is a weekly challenge?

A **weekly challenge** is a focused coding problem that can be solved in 1–3 hours. Each challenge includes:

- **Goal**: what needs to be built.
- **Requirements**: what the solution must satisfy.
- **Hints**: available if you get stuck.
- **Solutions**: community solutions are shared once the challenge wraps up.

!!! tip "How to join?"
    1. Read this week's challenge.
    2. Solve it in your editor (or in the [Playground](playground.md)).
    3. Share your solution in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/urukaisk-maker/Libro-desarrollador-Web-Urukais-/discussions).
    4. Compare it with other solutions.

## 🏆 How challenges are scored

Each challenge awards points based on difficulty:

| Level | Points | Estimated time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 Beginner | 10 XP | 30 min – 1 h |
| 🟡 Intermediate | 20 XP | 1–2 h |
| 🟠 Advanced | 30 XP | 2–4 h |
| 🔴 Professional | 50 XP | 4–8 h |

Points add up on your profile and count toward your level in the [achievements system](logros.md).
